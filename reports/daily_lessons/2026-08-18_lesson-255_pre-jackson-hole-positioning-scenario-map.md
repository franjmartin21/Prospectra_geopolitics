## Core Concept: Pre-Commitment as Edge

The standard investment mistake at high-visibility events (Fed speeches, elections, OPEC decisions) is **discretionary reaction**: watch the event, feel the sentiment, then decide. This sounds reasonable. It is actually the worst possible approach.

Why? Three reasons:

**1. Recency bias compounds on live TV.** When Powell fell 3.4% in 2022, the dominant instinct was "this is the end of the cycle — go full defensive." The right read was "the Fed just confirmed it's serious — inflation assets deflate, but the short-duration trade is now on." Most investors reacted to the emotion, not the information.

**2. Your reaction time is always behind the market's.** By the time you decide what a speech means, the first-order trade is already closed. The institutional desks had the play mapped in advance. You are competing with pre-committed decision trees, not live deliberation.

**3. The geopolitical layer adds a second source of confusion.** Jackson Hole 2026 lands in a world with: elevated US-China trade friction (tariff front still active), persistent BOJ normalization (the yen carry isn't fully unwound), energy market volatility (Middle East premium still embedded in crude), and EM currency stress. The speech will be interpreted through all these filters simultaneously. Without a pre-map, you will not know which signal to weight.

The solution is a **Scenario Matrix** with pre-defined response rules — built before the event, locked in, and only deviated from with an explicit written reason.

## Historical Grounding: Three Events That Punished Improvisation

**Jackson Hole 2022 — The Powell Pivot That Wasn't**
Markets had rallied 17% off June lows heading into Jackson Hole 2022, pricing a Fed pivot. Powell's eight-minute speech destroyed that thesis in one morning. The investors who were hurt were those who had not pre-committed to "if Powell fails to pivot, I reduce duration immediately." Instead they watched, felt uncertain, held, and rode the slide.

**ECB July 2012 — Draghi's "Whatever It Takes"**
This was the mirror image: a speech that was *more* aggressive than consensus expected. The investors who captured the full rally (Spanish and Italian spreads collapsed within days) were those who had a pre-committed rule: "if Draghi signals unlimited OMT backstop, buy peripheral sovereign debt immediately." Those who waited to "understand the implications" bought the second leg at a worse price.

**FOMC March 2020 — Sunday Emergency Cut**
Not Jackson Hole, but structurally identical: a high-impact central bank communication under geopolitical stress (COVID). The investors with pre-committed rules — "if the Fed goes emergency, buy TLT / GLD / VIX positions immediately" — captured the full tail-risk premium. Those who watched the news and waited missed the entry.

The pattern is consistent: **the edge is in the decision rule, not the interpretation speed.**

### Scenario A: Hawkish Surprise — "We Are Not Ready to Cut"
**What it looks like:** Warsh emphasizes labor market resilience, warns against premature easing, explicitly pushes back on the market's cut pricing. September meeting effectively taken off the table.

**First-order effects:**
- USD spikes (DXY +1.5-2.5%)
- Yields rise (2Y +15-25bps, 10Y +10-15bps)
- Equities drop (S&P -2 to -3%)
- EM FX under pressure (BRL, MXN, ZAR weakest)
- Gold down (vs. real rate move)
- BOJ carry unwind *pauses* — yen carry gets extended life

**Pre-committed response:**
- Reduce EM FX exposure (or hedge with short BRL NDF if held)
- Reduce long gold position (real rate headwind)
- Do NOT add equity shorts — this is a one-day shock, not a regime change
- Watch USD/JPY: if it breaks above 152 on Hawkish speech, that's a carry extension signal worth holding for 4-6 weeks

**What NOT to do:** Panic-buy duration as a "defensive" move. If Warsh is hawkish, long duration is exactly the wrong hedge.

---

### Scenario B: Dovish Pivot — "The Data Supports Easing"
**What it looks like:** Warsh confirms September cut, signals a "calibration cycle" of multiple modest reductions. Explicitly references the global disinflation trend. Markets price 3-4 cuts by end-2026.

**First-order effects:**
- USD weakens (DXY -1.5-2.0%)
- Yields fall (2Y -20-30bps, 10Y -10-20bps)
- Equities rally (S&P +1.5-2.5%, but tech leads, not defensives)
- Gold spikes (real rate drop + dollar weakness: the double catalyst)
- EM FX rallies (BRL, MXN, INR all benefit from capital inflow reversal)
- BOJ carry unwind *accelerates* — yen strengthens, USD/JPY drops toward 140

**Pre-committed response:**
- Add gold on initial spike if not already at full position
- EM equities: sector-selectively (India and Mexico benefit most from friendshoring + dollar weakness)
- Watch USD/JPY: if it breaks 144 on Dovish speech, that confirms carry unwind acceleration — add yen exposure
- Commodities: selective long copper (EM demand recovery signal), not broad commodity index

**What NOT to do:** Chase the equity rally in mega-cap US tech. That trade gets crowded fast and reverses within 2-3 weeks if the macro underlying doesn't confirm the easing story.

---

### Scenario C: Deliberate Ambiguity — "We Are Data-Dependent"
**What it looks like:** Warsh delivers a nuanced speech — acknowledges both inflation stickiness and growth slowdown, refuses to pre-commit to September, emphasizes meeting-by-meeting approach. A "nothing speech" by design.

**First-order effects:**
- Initial vol spike then compression (VIX up then down)
- USD modestly stronger (uncertainty premium)
- Short end of curve unchanged; long end mildly steeper
- Gold range-bound
- EM FX: modest weakness but not disorderly

**Pre-committed response:**
- Do nothing on the speech itself
- Use the vol compression post-speech as an entry point for: (1) long gold if you're underweight, (2) EM equity if you've been waiting for a cleaner entry
- The signal is not in the speech — it's in the September 17 FOMC dots
- Redirect attention: if Scenario C, the next actionable event is September CPI (September 10)

**What NOT to do:** Over-interpret the speech. Ambiguity is information: it means the Fed is genuinely uncertain. That is not a signal to act — it is a signal to wait.

---

### Scenario D: Geopolitical Override — Something Breaks Before August 27
**What it looks like:** A major exogenous event (military escalation, EM currency crisis, pipeline attack, China financial stress signal) forces the speech off the "macro normalization" script entirely.

**First-order effects:** Highly dependent on the nature of the event, but the default pattern is:
- USD spikes (flight to quality)
- Gold spikes (safe haven)
- Oil spikes (if Middle East) or drops (if demand destruction)
- EM FX collapses
- Warsh pivots speech to "we are monitoring closely" — September off the table regardless of prior signals

**Pre-committed response:**
- Gold position: hold or add (it's the clean geopolitical hedge)
- Reduce EM FX *before* the speech if GSI signals above 4.0 in the week of August 22
- This is where Pipeline 4 matters: you need the Signal 1 (BOJ) and Signal 2 (Middle East) reading from the pipeline *before* the speech to know which scenario is loading

**What NOT to do:** Sell equity blindly on geopolitical headlines. Geopolitical fear spikes are almost always entry points in diversified equity, not exit points. The exception is when the event directly threatens supply chains or payment infrastructure.

## Investment Implications: The Portfolio State Machine

The pre-commitment framework works best as a **state machine** — your portfolio has defined states, and the speech triggers a defined transition:

| Pre-Speech State | Scenario A (Hawk) | Scenario B (Dove) | Scenario C (Ambig) | Scenario D (Geo) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Underweight gold** | Hold underweight | Add to target | Add on weakness | Add immediately |
| **EM FX exposed** | Hedge or reduce | Hold/add | Monitor | Reduce before speech |
| **Long duration** | Reduce | Hold | Hold | Hold |
| **USD/JPY short (yen long)** | Exit | Hold/add | Hold | Hold |
| **EM equities** | Reduce | Add selectively | Monitor for entry | Hold |

The point of this table is that you define your *current state* before August 27, and the speech only changes your transition — it does not require you to rediscover your thesis on a live market day.
