## Core Concept: The Annual Threat Horizon Reset

Every serious intelligence operation — whether it runs at Langley, at a sovereign wealth fund's risk desk, or at a research house like ours — does a formal annual reset in Q4. The purpose is not to predict the year. It is to do three things:

1. **Audit the prior year's calls against outcomes** — not to celebrate wins but to identify where the framework mispriced, overweighted noise, or missed structural signals entirely.
2. **Map the 2027 event calendar** — identifying the known binary events (elections, treaty deadlines, fiscal cliffs, military thresholds) and the structural slow-burns that will compound regardless.
3. **Reposition the portfolio architecture** — not by chasing what happened, but by identifying the asymmetric opportunities that the 2026 framework has set up for 2027.

The discipline here is to treat November not as end-of-year clean-up but as the best intelligence window of the year. Markets are still liquid. December brings thin tape and year-end distortions. November is when the framework should do its hardest work.

### Historical Grounding: The 2010 Template

In late 2010, the structural intelligence was clear to anyone who looked: European sovereign debt crisis was not resolved, it was postponed. The Greek bailout of May 2010 had bought 18 months. Portugal, Ireland, Spain, and potentially Italy were structurally exposed. PIGS spreads had widened, then compressed on the bailout announcement, then started widening again.

The investors who reset their framework in November 2010 — identifying the Q1 2011 Portuguese crisis and the Q3 2011 Italian contagion as near-certainties — were positioned before the events. They were short Italian BTP futures, long German Bund futures, long CDS on Spanish sovereigns. They were not reacting. They had done the annual reset.

The investors who didn't do the reset were still congratulating themselves on navigating 2010 when the 2011 crises arrived. Same playbook, different timing.

The lesson: **The annual reset is not a retrospective exercise. It is a prospective positioning tool.**

## 2026 Track Record Audit — CEO Assessment

### What the Framework Got Right

1. **Japan carry unwind risk** (Lessons 191, 426): The BOJ rate normalization thesis — that an end to negative rates would trigger a global yen carry unwind — played out in stages throughout 2026. Our directional call (long JPY, short carry-funded EM assets on BOJ signals) was correct directionally. The *magnitude* of the first unwind wave (August 2026) exceeded our base case by approximately 30%.

2. **Fiscal dominance arc** (Lessons 72, 148, 427-439): The thesis that rising US debt service costs would begin constraining fiscal flexibility — and that bond vigilantes would return — proved more prescient than consensus. The term premium on 10-year USTs has risen meaningfully in 2026. Our real assets / TIPS / gold positioning as a fiscal dominance hedge has performed.

3. **Gulf energy security** (Lessons 197-209): The Iran-related Hormuz disruption risk kept energy elevated throughout 2026, in line with our multi-scenario framework. We identified the range ($85-115 Brent) as the managed-conflict equilibrium and it largely held.

### What the Framework Mispriced

1. **China hard landing timing**: We flagged China's internal fragility (demographics, property, debt) as a 2026 risk. It has been a slow-burn, not an acute crisis. The timeline is longer than the 12-18 month investment horizon we anchored to. Recalibration required.

2. **Dollar resilience under fiscal pressure**: The fiscal dominance framework correctly identified the *risk* of dollar weakness, but the dollar has remained stronger than our base case. Exorbitant privilege is more durable than the structural thesis alone suggests. The dollar milkshake dynamic (global dollar shortage in a risk-off environment) is a competing force that the framework has underweighted.

3. **European defense ramp speed**: Our thesis on European defense spending was directionally correct (defense stocks outperformed), but we underestimated how quickly Germany's 100bn fund would be deployed and how that would accelerate the European defense industrial thesis. We were right but under-sized.

- **Critical minerals consolidation**: The rare earth / gallium / germanium export control game is entering a new phase where the US-EU-Japan axis is building strategic reserves. This is a multi-year supply chain disruption with 2027 inflection points.
