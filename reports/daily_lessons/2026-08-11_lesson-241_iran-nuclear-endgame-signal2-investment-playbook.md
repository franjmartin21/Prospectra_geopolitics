### The Key Technical Facts (Investment-Relevant Only)

**Enrichment levels and their significance:**

| Level | Use | Iran's Status |
|---|---|---|
| 3.67% | Civilian power reactors | Compliant pre-JCPOA |
| 20% | Medical isotopes / research | Iran crossed this in 2021 |
| 60% | No legitimate civilian use | Iran crossed this in 2021 |
| **90%+** | **Weapons-grade** | **Current assessed: Iran stockpile near breakout capacity** |

The **breakout time** — how long it would take Iran to produce enough weapons-grade uranium for one device — has been assessed by IAEA at **under two weeks** as of mid-2025. By August 2026, that window may have narrowed further as centrifuge cascades at Fordow and Natanz continue operating.

**What "crossing the threshold" means in practice:**
Iran possessing weapons-grade material in sufficient quantity does not automatically mean Iran has a deployable weapon. A deliverable device requires:
- Weaponization (miniaturization, trigger assembly) — likely 6–18 months additional
- A delivery vehicle (Iran has ballistic missiles, so this constraint may already be met)
- A decision by the Supreme Leader to actually proceed

The market cares about **the announcement of enrichment to 90%** (or a credible leak), not about whether Iran has a tested device. The political and strategic consequence of confirmed weapons-grade production is what triggers the market response.

The Biden-era JCPOA revival effort never concluded, and the Trump 2.0 administration's "maximum pressure reloaded" posture has increased sanctions pressure without producing a deal.

The key dynamic: Iran needs sanctions relief to stabilize its economy. The US needs Iran to freeze enrichment. Neither side has moved enough to satisfy the other. This is a frozen conflict with rising stakes as Iran's technical capabilities improve every quarter.

**The critical point about Phase 1:** Do not buy oil stocks in Phase 1. The spike is emotional. The smart money that positioned before the event is selling into the initial spike. Phase 1 is for existing positions to deliver, not for new entries.

### Phase 2: Reality Check (Days 3–14)

The market reprices from "emotional shock" to "what are the actual supply implications?"

**Key questions the market resolves in Phase 2:**
- Does the US military strike Iran's enrichment facilities? (If yes: oil spikes further. If no: some reversal.)
- Does Israel strike independently? (Higher probability than US strike; lower market impact but ongoing volatility)
- Does the Strait of Hormuz actually get threatened or closed?
- How does Saudi Arabia respond? (Saudi production hike to offset Iranian disruption or political statement?)

**Historical analog:** The January 2020 Soleimani assassination. Brent spiked 4% within hours, then reversed entirely within 5 days as it became clear neither side wanted full escalation. This is the Phase 1 → Phase 2 reversal pattern.

**The difference in the nuclear scenario:** A nuclear threshold crossing cannot be as easily dismissed as a kinetic event. The strategic posture change is structural, not episodic. Phase 2 may not produce a full reversal.

### Phase 3: New Equilibrium (Weeks 2–12)

**Structural repricing (if Hormuz is not physically blocked):**
- Brent at $90–$110: sustained risk premium reflecting permanent strategic uncertainty
- LNG shipping rates elevated: rerouting adds 3–5 days to Middle East → Europe voyages
- Israeli defense posture shifts: increased spending, political volatility
- US-Saudi relations stressed: Saudi Arabia faces impossible choices between US alignment and Iran containment

**Structural repricing (if Hormuz is physically blocked — the 10% tail within Scenario C):**
- Brent at $130–$160+: full Hormuz closure removes ~20% of global oil supply
- Global recession risk: oil price shock of this magnitude triggers demand destruction
- This is the "2008 oil shock meets 2022 Russia energy shock" scenario
- Portfolio action: This is the scenario where energy equities briefly spike then fall with the broader market, and defensive positioning (gold, short-duration bonds, cash) dominates

## 6. Databricks Angle — Signal 2 Data Architecture

### Existing Infrastructure You Can Extend

Pipeline 1 (GDELT) already captures Iran-related event volume via the `EventBaseCode` and `Actor1Code` filters. To enhance it for Signal 2:

```python
# Add to Pipeline 1 GDELT Query — Iran Nuclear Theme Filter
# GDELT GKG Theme code for nuclear: 'WB_2719_NUCLEAR_SECURITY'
# GDELT Actor1Code for Iran: 'IRN'

spark.sql("""
  SELECT 
    DATE(DATEADDED / 1000000) AS event_date,
    COUNT(*) AS event_count,
    AVG(Tone) AS avg_tone,
    SUM(CASE WHEN Themes LIKE '%NUCLEAR%' THEN 1 ELSE 0 END) AS nuclear_mention_count
  FROM prospectra.bronze.gdelt_events  
  WHERE Actor1Code = 'IRN' 
    OR Actor2Code = 'IRN'
  GROUP BY 1
  ORDER BY 1 DESC
  LIMIT 90
""")
```

### New Data Inputs for Pipeline 2-B

```python
import yfinance as yf
import pandas as pd

def fetch_iran_risk_indicators():
    """
    Pulls financial market indicators that serve as Iran nuclear risk proxies.
    All data via yfinance — no additional dependencies.
    """
    indicators = {}
    
    # OVX — Oil volatility index (CBOE)
    ovx = yf.Ticker("^OVX")
    ovx_hist = ovx.history(period="30d")
    if not ovx_hist.empty:
        indicators["ovx"] = {
            "current": ovx_hist["Close"].iloc[-1],
            "30d_avg": ovx_hist["Close"].mean(),
            "threshold_breach": ovx_hist["Close"].iloc[-1] > 40
        }
    
    # ILS/USD — Israeli shekel
    ils = yf.Ticker("ILS=X")
    ils_hist = ils.history(period="14d")
    if not ils_hist.empty:
        ils_change_5d = ((ils_hist["Close"].iloc[-1] - ils_hist["Close"].iloc[-5]) 
                        / ils_hist["Close"].iloc[-5] * 100)
        indicators["ils"] = {
            "spot": ils_hist["Close"].iloc[-1],
            "change_5d_pct": ils_change_5d,
            # Note: ILS/USD — higher number = ILS weaker = more risk
            "threshold_breach": ils_change_5d > 2.0  
        }
    
    # ESLT — Elbit Systems (Israeli defense)
    eslt = yf.Ticker("ESLT")
    eslt_hist = eslt.history(period="30d")
    if not eslt_hist.empty:
        recent_vol = eslt_hist["Volume"].iloc[-3:].mean()
        avg_vol = eslt_hist["Volume"].mean()
        vol_ratio = recent_vol / avg_vol if avg_vol > 0 else 1.0
        indicators["eslt"] = {
            "volume_ratio": vol_ratio,
            "threshold_breach": vol_ratio > 2.0
        }
    
    return indicators
```

### Feature Engineering: Cross-Signal Regime Detection

Once Pipeline 2-A (BOJ) and Pipeline 2-B (Iran) are both live and writing to `signal_calibration_log`, you can compute the **Geopolitical Stress Index** — the first true cross-domain signal:

```sql
-- Weekly batch: Geopolitical Stress Index
SELECT 
  run_date,
  MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2a_boj_monitor' THEN raw_score END) AS boj_score,
  MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2b_iran_monitor' THEN raw_score END) AS iran_score,
  MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_4_export_control' THEN raw_score END) AS export_score,
  
  -- Composite: max of (BOJ, Iran) represents systemic tail risk
  -- Export score represents structural tech decoupling
  GREATEST(
    MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2a_boj_monitor' THEN raw_score END),
    MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2b_iran_monitor' THEN raw_score END)
  ) AS tail_risk_component,
  
  -- Weighted composite
  (
    0.35 * MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2a_boj_monitor' THEN raw_score END) +
    0.35 * MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2b_iran_monitor' THEN raw_score END) +
    0.30 * MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_4_export_control' THEN raw_score END)
  ) AS geopolitical_stress_index,
  
  CASE 
    WHEN GREATEST(boj_score, iran_score) >= 4.5 THEN 'ACUTE_TAIL_RISK'
    WHEN GREATEST(boj_score, iran_score) >= 4.0 THEN 'ELEVATED_TAIL_RISK'
    WHEN export_score >= 4.0 THEN 'STRUCTURAL_DECOUPLING'
    ELSE 'NORMAL'
  END AS portfolio_regime

FROM prospectra.gold.signal_calibration_log
GROUP BY run_date
ORDER BY run_date DESC
```

## 7. The Asymmetry That Makes Signal 2 Different from Signal 1

This is the conceptual point that deserves to be separated from the operational playbook.

**Signal 1 (BOJ) is a continuous, gradual process:**
- Rate hikes are announced with 1–7 day warning (BOJ policy meetings are scheduled)
- The market adjusts in real-time to rate expectations via OIS pricing
- Pipeline 2-A gives you probabilistic early warning that improves over 2–4 weeks
- The event is *priceable in advance* because the distribution of outcomes is narrow

**Signal 2 (Iran nuclear) is a discontinuous, binary event:**
- There is no "Iran nuclear-lite" — either the threshold is crossed or it is not
- The event may be covert (Iran does not announce it; an intelligence agency leaks it or IAEA detects it)
- The market goes from "25% probability" to "100% certainty" in hours, not days
- Pre-positioning is the only risk management available — there is no warning window to trade into

**The implication for portfolio construction:**
You cannot hedge against Signal 2 in real-time. You can only be pre-positioned before it.

The current portfolio is ~70% protected for Scenario C through energy (XOM, CVX, LNG) and gold (GLD). The tanker gap (identified above) is the key remaining exposure. At 1–2% portfolio sizing in tanker equities, the cost of carrying that pre-hedge is minimal — approximately equivalent to the insurance premium you pay on a car you might not crash.

The CEO recommendation is clear: **add tanker exposure (FRO or DHT) before October.** Not because Scenario C is likely — 55% probability still goes to stalemate. But because the cost of the pre-hedge is asymmetric: low cost if wrong, meaningful protection if right.
