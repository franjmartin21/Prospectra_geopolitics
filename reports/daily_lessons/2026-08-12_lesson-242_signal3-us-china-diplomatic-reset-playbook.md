## 2. What "US-China Diplomatic Reset" Actually Means — Defining Signal 3

The first problem with Signal 3 is definitional vagueness. "US-China reset" could mean anything from a bilateral phone call to a structural dismantling of the decoupling architecture. The investment implications are radically different across that spectrum. So we define it precisely.

### The Signal 3 Trigger Hierarchy

**Level 1 — Cosmetic Thaw (Not Signal 3)**
- Top-level diplomatic meeting (Xi-Trump bilateral call or summit)
- Resumption of military-to-military communication channels
- Student visa policy normalization

*Market impact: short-lived relief rally in Chinese equities (+3–5%), no structural repricing. This is noise, not signal. Do not act.*

**Level 2 — Partial De-escalation (Signal 3 Watch Mode)**
- Rollback of tariffs imposed after 2022 (returning to 2022 baseline, not 2018)
- Restoration of some semiconductor export licenses for non-advanced chips (e.g., lifting NVIDIA H100 ban but maintaining Blackwell/B200 restrictions)
- Taiwan diplomatic communiqué affirming "one China" framework without new coercive language

*Market impact: meaningful. Chinese tech re-rates +10–20%. Taiwan risk premium partially deflates. This is real. Begin partial Signal 3 response.*

**Level 3 — Structural Reset (Full Signal 3)**
- Tariff framework agreement: phase-down timeline from current 145% average → 30% or below within 24 months
- Semiconductor sector carve-out: advanced chip licensing restored on a case-by-case basis (not blanket, but systematic)
- BIS Entity List removals: at least 5 major Chinese AI companies removed from the restricted list
- Taiwan confidence-building measures: PLA withdrawal of recent escalation postures

*Market impact: regime-change level. Full Signal 3 playbook triggers. This is the scenario that produces 25–40% moves in some asset classes over 3–6 months.*

**CEO Signal 3 Definition for Playbook Purposes:**
Signal 3 triggers at Level 2 or above. Level 1 is noise. Level 2 triggers Signal 3 Watch Mode. Level 3 triggers the full playbook.

## 3. Current Probability Assessment — August 2026

*This is the CEO's view. It is explicit, falsifiable, and will be updated as conditions change.*

### Why Signal 3 Has Non-Zero Probability

**The economic constraint on both sides is real.**

The US economy in August 2026 is experiencing:
- Persistent core inflation partly driven by import price pass-through from 145% China tariffs
- Weakening consumer sentiment as durable goods prices (electronics, furniture, appliances) remain elevated
- A fiscal position that is structurally challenged — tariff revenue helps the deficit, but domestic manufacturing relocation costs are not creating the political dividends promised

China's economy in August 2026 is experiencing:
- Property sector stabilization at a permanently lower level (not recovery — stabilization)
- Youth unemployment at structural highs
- Export growth dependent on non-US markets that are increasingly capacity-constrained
- Deflationary pressure that requires demand stimulus the domestic economy cannot provide on its own

**Both governments face electoral or legitimacy pressure to show economic results.** In a world where both sides have economic incentives to de-escalate, the binary framing of "permanent decoupling" underestimates optionality.

**The precedent from 2018–2020 is informative:**
The US-China trade war of 2018–2019 was followed by the Phase One deal of January 2020. The Phase One deal was widely dismissed as unserious (it was largely unimplemented). But it produced a meaningful market re-rating of Chinese equities (+15% in six months) even without structural change. Level 2 de-escalation does not need to be transformative to produce significant market impact.

### The Probability Distribution

| Scenario | CEO Probability | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **A: Continued decoupling — no reset** | 55% | Export controls tighten further, tariffs remain, Taiwan tension elevated. Pipeline 4 baseline. |
| **B: Level 2 partial de-escalation** | 30% | Tariff rollbacks in some categories, semiconductor licensing partial restoration, diplomatic channel active. |
| **C: Level 3 structural reset** | 15% | Full trade framework, export control overhaul, Taiwan communiqué. Requires political conditions that don't currently exist. |

**Key point: Signal 3 triggers at Level 2 or above. Combined probability = 45%.**

This is higher than most market participants are pricing. Chinese equities trade at 8–12x earnings versus US equities at 22–25x — the magnitude of the discount implies the market has priced decoupling as near-permanent, not as a 55% base case. There is asymmetric mispricing here.

**What could shift the probability rapidly:**

*Toward Scenario C (would raise the probability from 15% → 30%+):*
- US CPI print showing tariffs as primary inflation driver (creates political imperative to cut)
- Chinese concession on Fentanyl/carfentanil trafficking enforcement (Trump's stated precondition)

## 10. Databricks Angle — Pipeline 3 Data Architecture

Pipeline 3 is the simplest of the three signal pipelines from a technical standpoint. It shares infrastructure with Pipeline 2-A (yfinance) and Pipeline 4 (GDELT + news scraping).

### New Input: KWEB/SPY Relative Performance

```python
import yfinance as yf
import pandas as pd
from datetime import date

def fetch_china_reset_market_signals():
    """
    Pulls market-based signals for US-China reset probability.
    Uses KWEB/SPY ratio as primary market-implied reset indicator.
    """
    signals = {}
    
    # KWEB/SPY ratio — primary market signal
    kweb = yf.Ticker("KWEB")
    spy = yf.Ticker("SPY")
    
    kweb_hist = kweb.history(period="90d")
    spy_hist = spy.history(period="90d")
    
    if not kweb_hist.empty and not spy_hist.empty:
        # Align on common dates
        kweb_close = kweb_hist["Close"].rename("kweb")
        spy_close = spy_hist["Close"].rename("spy")
        combined = pd.concat([kweb_close, spy_close], axis=1).dropna()
        
        # Compute rolling ratio
        combined["ratio"] = combined["kweb"] / combined["spy"]
        
        current_ratio = combined["ratio"].iloc[-1]
        ratio_30d_ago = combined["ratio"].iloc[-30] if len(combined) >= 30 else combined["ratio"].iloc[0]
        ratio_change_30d = ((current_ratio - ratio_30d_ago) / ratio_30d_ago) * 100
        
        print(f"KWEB/SPY ratio: {current_ratio:.4f}")
        print(f"30-day change: {ratio_change_30d:.2f}%")
        
        # A rising KWEB/SPY ratio signals the market is pricing in China reset
        signals["kweb_spy"] = {
            "ratio": current_ratio,
            "change_30d_pct": ratio_change_30d,
            "threshold_breach": ratio_change_30d > 10.0  # >10% in 30d = Watch Mode
        }
    
    # CNH/USD — yuan strengthening as capital flow normalization signal
    cnh = yf.Ticker("CNH=X")
    cnh_hist = cnh.history(period="30d")
    
    if not cnh_hist.empty:
        current_cnh = cnh_hist["Close"].iloc[-1]
        cnh_30d_ago = cnh_hist["Close"].iloc[-30] if len(cnh_hist) >= 30 else cnh_hist["Close"].iloc[0]
        cnh_change = ((current_cnh - cnh_30d_ago) / cnh_30d_ago) * 100
        
        # CNH=X: higher number = MORE yuan per dollar = WEAKER yuan
        # We want to detect yuan strengthening (lower CNH/USD ratio)
        yuan_strengthening = cnh_change < -1.5  # Yuan strengthening >1.5% in 30 days
        
        print(f"CNH/USD: {current_cnh:.4f} | 30d change: {cnh_change:.2f}%")
        
        signals["cnh"] = {
            "spot": current_cnh,
            "change_30d_pct": cnh_change,
            "threshold_breach": yuan_strengthening
        }
    
    return signals
```

     OR (Actor1Code = 'CHN' AND Actor2Code = 'USA')
  GROUP BY 1
  ORDER BY 1 DESC
  LIMIT 90
""")

### The Geopolitical Stress Index — Four-Input Final Version

Once Pipeline 3 is live (October 31), the cross-signal composite becomes a true four-input system:

```sql
-- Geopolitical Stress Index v2.0 — Final Architecture
SELECT 
  run_date,
  MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2a_boj_monitor' THEN raw_score END) AS boj_score,
  MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2b_iran_monitor' THEN raw_score END) AS iran_score,
  MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_4_export_control' THEN raw_score END) AS export_score,
  MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_3_china_reset' THEN raw_score END) AS china_reset_score,
  
  -- Composite: weighted average
  -- Note: china_reset_score is INVERTED — higher reset score = lower stress
  (
    0.30 * MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2a_boj_monitor' THEN raw_score END) +
    0.30 * MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_2b_iran_monitor' THEN raw_score END) +
    0.25 * MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_4_export_control' THEN raw_score END) +
    0.15 * (6 - MAX(CASE WHEN pipeline_name = 'pipeline_3_china_reset' THEN raw_score END))
    -- Inversion: china_reset score 5 (full reset) → stress contribution = 1 (low)
    -- china_reset score 1 (full decoupling) → stress contribution = 5 (high)
  ) AS geopolitical_stress_index,
  
  -- Regime classification
  CASE
    WHEN GREATEST(boj_score, iran_score) >= 4.5 THEN 'ACUTE_TAIL_RISK'
    WHEN GREATEST(boj_score, iran_score) >= 4.0 THEN 'ELEVATED_TAIL_RISK'
    WHEN china_reset_score >= 4.5 THEN 'ASIA_RISK_ON' -- reset scenario
    WHEN export_score >= 4.0 AND china_reset_score < 3.0 THEN 'STRUCTURAL_DECOUPLING'
    ELSE 'NORMAL'
  END AS portfolio_regime

FROM prospectra.gold.signal_calibration_log
GROUP BY run_date
ORDER BY run_date DESC
```

**Note on the inversion:** The China reset score works in the *opposite direction* from the other three signals. A high BOJ score = danger. A high Iran score = danger. A high export control score = danger. But a high China reset score = *opportunity*, which means *lower* systemic stress. The inversion formula `(6 - score)` converts it to a consistent "stress contribution" scale.
