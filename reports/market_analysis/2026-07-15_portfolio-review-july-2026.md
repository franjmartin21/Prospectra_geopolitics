**The CEO's honest assessment:** The portfolio is correctly positioned for this environment. The structural forces that built these positions — fiscal dominance, fragmentation, rearmament, energy security premium — are all running at elevated intensity simultaneously. The challenge is not whether the thesis is right; it is managing the volatility of being right in an environment where the same structural forces create enormous short-term price swings that can trigger forced selling in less disciplined frameworks.

The discipline is: when the position is right for the right reasons and no falsification condition has triggered, hold. Add on dislocations. Trim only when a structural condition has materially changed.

That is the framework. That is the discipline. That is what 170 lessons built toward.
