## The Critical Problem

The Prospectra analytical platform — the GDELT pipeline, the Market Data Feed,
the Geopolitical Risk Index — was the Phase 1-3 Databricks build target.
It was due by July 2026. It is not built.

The investment log is full of entries that say "tag for Databricks once B-02 is live"
and "wire into market data feed" and "build Sahel uranium supply risk tracker."
These have been accumulating for 5 months. None are done.

The platform is the only thing that separates this project from "a person with
good geopolitical intuition" to "a systematic signal engine." Without it:
- The investment calls cannot be validated at scale
- The GRI does not exist as a product
- Goal 3 has nothing to demonstrate to a Series A investor
- The thesis has no data backbone

## CEO Decision

### 1. Curriculum redirect — effective immediately

Lessons 377 onward shift format:

**Build-first, then study.** Each session starts with a Databricks deliverable
check (confirmed by git commit), then delivers the next lesson. No commit = no
lesson. The CEO is not softening this.

The next lesson topic (377) is: **Databricks Platform Architecture Review —
Mapping What Exists to What Was Promised.** Bolo reads his own databricks/ folder,
git logs it, and produces a written gap analysis. This is the pre-condition for
advancing the Series A curriculum.

### 2. Hard deadline: October 2026 Databricks sprint

**Target: Phase 1 complete by October 31, 2026.**

Phase 1 deliverables (overdue from Week 3 of the original mandate):
- B-01: GDELT event feed → daily event counts by country (10 countries minimum)
- B-02: Market data feed → daily prices for oil, gold, copper, wheat, EUR/USD, TRY/USD
- B-03: Basic correlation engine → lag analysis between GDELT score and asset price

These are not research projects. They are data plumbing tasks. A Databricks
Solutions Architect can complete all three in a focused 2-week sprint.

If these are not in git by October 31, the CEO re-evaluates the pace of the
entire project and the viability of the 12-week commercialization track.

### 3. European industrials short — close in October as committed

Hard close date is October 2026. This is non-negotiable. The position was right.
Execute the close, log the outcome, and book the discipline of honoring pre-committed
exit rules as a framework win.

### 4. UNGA tail hedge — closed (logged separately in investment_log.md)

Expired worthless September 26 as predicted. Framework grade: A.

without a product. Before continuing that track, Bolo reads:

*Superforecasting: The Art and Science of Prediction* — Philip Tetlock & Dan Gardner

Reason: Lesson 376 studied how to hire analytical alpha. Tetlock is the empirical

Reason: The commercial framing for what Prospectra is building. Intelligence

commercial reality.

## What the CEO Got Wrong

The lesson curriculum expansion to 376 lessons — including the startup operations
arc — was a CEO decision. It was not mandated by Bolo. I own that decision.

The expansion produced genuine analytical depth. Lessons on currency crises,
financial repression, central bank independence, the Eurodollar system, and
BRICS de-dollarization are all high-quality and investment-relevant.

But the CEO made an error in not coupling each lesson expansion with a
corresponding Databricks build deliverable. The learning compounded; the building
did not. That asymmetry is corrected starting today.

The Series A curriculum was premature by approximately 3-4 months. There is
nothing wrong with studying it, but the CEO should not have let it substitute
for progress on the build. That is on the CEO.
